Whether cable concealment is a weekend DIY job or a call to a professional mostly comes down to whether the cord needs to go inside the wall or just along it. The cost gap between the two is bigger than most people expect.

## Surface-Level Concealment: DIY Territory

Anything that routes along the wall rather than through it — a paintable cord hider wall channel, adhesive clips, a baseboard cord cover — is a DIY job with materials typically running $15–$60 depending on run length. No electrician, no wall-cutting, and most kits install in under an hour.

## In-Wall Cable Routing: Where Professional Costs Start

Running a cord through the wall cavity itself, rather than along the surface, is a different job entirely. It usually means fishing cable through insulation, cutting low-voltage wall plates at both ends, and sometimes coordinating with an outlet relocation. Professional in-wall TV cable installs commonly run $150–$400 depending on wall type and distance, and that's before any electrical work like adding a new outlet behind the TV.

## When DIY Isn't Actually Cheaper

If a DIY in-wall attempt goes wrong — a cord snagged in insulation, a wall cut in the wrong spot — the repair cost can exceed what professional installation would have cost from the start. In-wall work is the one category on this list where the DIY savings are smallest relative to the risk.

## A Middle Option

For a lot of TV setups, a surface channel handles the visible run and an outlet-level fix like a <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GS4W3D7J" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">cable-management outlet cover</a> handles the cluttered plug end — both DIY, both under $50 combined, and both avoid the wall-cutting step that drives professional pricing up.

## The Bottom Line

If the cord can run along the wall, do it yourself — the tools and kits are inexpensive and low-risk. If it needs to go inside the wall, get a quote before starting; a botched in-wall attempt often costs more to fix than professional installation would have in the first place.
